# Proposed hospital preparedness pilot

**Decision requested.** Identify two or three preparedness or exercise-review stakeholders and authorize discovery for one suitable dataset. Obtain a reviewer independent of the product team to adjudicate the evidence. No participant study or pilot measurement has been performed.

## Study design

1. Agree on an exercise with permitted primary records, objectives and time targets. Define scope, privacy review, source rights, roles, retention and access before any transfer. Prefer a completed exercise with de-identified source material and a manageable review scope.
2. Describe the current manual workflow and define a common output covering objectives, supported findings, source locators, unknowns, actions and draft AAR/IP. Agree on exclusions, effort logging, adjudication labels and numeric go/no-go thresholds before examining outputs.
3. Use the same source corpus and objectives in both conditions. Divide comparable objectives into two sets and counterbalance the order across evaluators to reduce carryover. Record familiarity, tool training, task order and setup time.
4. Give an independent evaluator outputs with anonymous condition labels where practical. Check source wording, locator, semantic support, unknowns, action traceability and decision state. Record an adjudication note and retain disagreements.
5. Compare paired effort and quality per participant and objective set. Report raw counts and distributions. A sample this small is a directional study.

| KPI | Collection and denominator |
| --- | --- |
| Preparation effort | Active minutes from starting review to first draft for each completed reviewer-condition pair. Record setup and idle time separately. |
| Verification effort | Active minutes checking source support and reconciling disputes per reviewed finding and per session. |
| Finding quality | Supported findings divided by adjudicated proposals. Compute recall only with an exhaustive independently labeled reference set. Report unsupported and partial counts. |
| Citation fidelity | Correct source bytes, locator and wording divided by checked citations. Assess semantic support separately and preserve adjudicated uncertainty. |
| Reviewer corrections | Counts per reviewed finding for factual correction, added evidence, changed or withdrawn interpretation, and corrected owner or date. |
| Improvement traceability | Actions with verified finding link, accepted owner, target, acceptance criterion and reviewer decision divided by eligible actions. Report closure evidence for actions actually reviewed for closure. |
| Workflow controls | Approval or closure bypasses, cross-workspace accesses, unsupported factual claims and disclosure events per respective check. |
| Operational friction | Upload or export failures, OCR quality exceptions, session interruptions, AI latency and minutes spent reviewing AI suggestions. |

**Go/no-go.** A go decision requires zero observed approval or closure bypasses, zero unresolved privacy violations, evidence quality meeting the agreed minimum, and paired effort improvement meeting the agreed threshold without an increase in serious corrections. A borderline result triggers narrower reassessment. Failure of the safety or evidence-quality minimum stops expansion pending remediation. Agree on numeric thresholds before timing and adjudication begin.

**Dependencies.** Hospital or coalition authorization, approved handling and retention, an independent evaluator, participants with time for both conditions, and genuine adjudicable sources. A deployment beyond the local prototype needs a separate approval and security decision.
