## Decision handling

The engineering team can implement against approved decisions but must not infer or silently select them. Record the decision, approver role/name, date, rationale, and affected configuration in the organization's controlled decision system. Revisit the gate documents after decisions are made. Until then, use fictional evidence in isolated local demos only.
